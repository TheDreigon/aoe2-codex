# Out of scope

These civs exist in Age of Empires II but do not pass the project's [definition of a civ](RULES.md#3-what-a-civ-is). On the site they appear in a separate section, with their original design and no rework. This is not a judgement on how well they were made: some have well-documented elements, which we reuse in other civs.

| Civ | Why | What we reuse |
|---|---|---|
| **Wei** (220–266) | One of three rival regimes of the same Han people, lasting 46 years, in the 3rd century, before the 500–1500 time frame. Fails the identity, continuity and time-frame tests. | The Tuntian (military-agricultural colonies, later reused by the Tang and by the Ming *weisuo* system). The Traction Trebuchet, the typical Chinese artillery until 1273. |
| **Shu Han** (221–263) | Same case, 42 years. | The repeating crossbow associated with Zhuge Liang (the Chu Ko Nu is already Chinese). |
| **Wu** (222–280) | Same case, 58 years. | The naval and river tradition, which fits the Song standing navy. |
| **Romans** | The 4th–5th-century Western Empire predates the time frame. The medieval Romans are already represented by the Byzantines. | To be decided. |

The heroes of the Three Kingdoms civs have no place in a civ design either: civs represent peoples, not individual characters.
