%%CROSS%%

Method and legend

    %%LEGEND%%

Data sources: bonuses, tech trees and descriptions read from data/data.json and data/locales/en/strings.json in the aoe2techtree repository (commit 3bb43b1, “Add The Viking Sagas”). The game’s history texts were taken from the Age of Empires wiki (fandom), History section of each civ.

Process: 11 regional historians analysed the civs in groups; each analysis was reviewed by a skeptical checker who corrected facts, recalibrated scores and added omissions. Four thematic analyses went through the same process. The synthesis was written from the verified results.

Limits: the fidelity scores are judgements, not measurements. The history texts are paraphrased. Points debated among historians are marked as such in each civ.
